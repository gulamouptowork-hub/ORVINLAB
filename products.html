Four services, and what's included in each.

No packages, no tiers, no "contact us for enterprise". Here is what each service includes, how it works, and what you keep at the end.

01 · Websites

A site that loads before anyone loses interest.

Phones first Fast load times You edit the copy Domain in your name

What it is

Most small-business sites lose people before they finish rendering. Ours are built lean on purpose — the page arrives in under a second on a phone on mobile data, and it says what you sell above the fold instead of below a stock photo of a handshake.

It's also yours to run. Prices, hours, photos and text live in a plain editor, so a Tuesday price change is a Tuesday price change, not a support ticket and a three-day wait.

What you get

  • Up to 6 pages, written with you
  • Mobile-first build, tested on real phones
  • Editor access, plus a walkthrough
  • Contact and enquiry forms, tested by hand
  • Analytics and search setup
  • Domain and hosting in your name

02 · Advertising

Spend aimed only at people already looking.

Google & Meta Media billed at cost Call tracking Your ad account

What it is

We start deliberately small — one or two campaigns, tight geography, aimed at the searches that mean someone is ready to buy rather than idly browsing. Then we watch which ones produce real enquiries and move the money there.

You pay Google and Meta directly and we charge for the managing. That means we gain nothing from talking you into a bigger budget, and when something stops paying we say so instead of quietly leaving it running.

What you get

  • Ad accounts opened in your name
  • Keyword and audience research
  • Ad copy written and tested
  • A landing page matched to each ad
  • Call and form tracking, per source
  • One readable page of results a month

03 · Automation

The jobs you do twice, done once.

Quotes Bookings Invoice chasing Follow-ups

What it is

Every small business has four or five things that get retyped every week — the same quote, the same booking confirmation, the third reminder about an unpaid invoice. Individually they're two minutes. Together they're most of a working day you never billed anyone for.

We find them, then remove them one at a time. Nothing goes out automatically that shouldn't — anything a customer will read, you approve first, until you trust it enough not to.

What you get

  • A map of where your week actually goes
  • Self-pricing quote or booking form
  • Automatic confirmations and reminders
  • Invoice and deposit chasing
  • Follow-ups on a schedule you set
  • Plain-English notes on changing it yourself

04 · AI

It answers in your words, and you approve first.

Trained on your answers Enquiry sorting Draft replies You approve

What it is

Not a chatbot bolted to the corner of your homepage. We take the twenty questions you genuinely answer every week — opening hours, delivery areas, whether you do that one awkward size — and let something handle them in your own phrasing, drawn from how you already reply.

On the inbox side it reads incoming enquiries, flags the ones worth your morning, and drafts the dull replies for you to send or bin. Nothing reaches a customer that you haven't seen.

What you get

  • An assistant trained on your real answers
  • Site widget or inbox integration
  • Enquiry sorting and priority flags
  • Draft replies held for your approval
  • A hand-off rule for anything unusual
  • Monthly review of what it got wrong
