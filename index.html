How it goes

Four steps, and you always know which one you're in.

The whole thing is designed to end with you not needing us. That last step is the point of the first three.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    One call, 30 minutes. What you sell, who buys it, and where your week actually goes. You leave with a plan whether or not you hire us.

  2. 02

    Build

    A live link in week one that you watch fill in. Comment on it whenever you like — no month of silence ending in a you have to be polite about.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Tested on real phones, every form submitted once by a human, tracking live from hour one. Day one already tells you something.

  4. 04

    Autonomy

    We hand over the logins, walk you through editing it, and send one readable page a month. Stay if it's useful. Leave and nothing breaks.

Common questions

The three things everybody asks first.

A site for a small business usually lands between a few hundred and a few thousand, depending on how many pages and how much of it we write. Ads are billed at cost — you pay Google or Meta directly and we charge for the managing, so we never profit from talking you into a bigger budget. You'll have a number in writing before anyone starts, and it doesn't move unless you ask for something new.

No — it's most of who we work with. You'll never be asked to choose between two hosting plans or told something is "just a DNS thing." We handle the plumbing, and the only part you touch is a text box where you change your prices. If a sentence we send you needs a second read, that's our mistake to fix, not yours to decode.

You take everything with you, because it was already yours. The domain, hosting, ad accounts and data are registered in your name on day one, not ours — so leaving is a password change, not a negotiation. There's no minimum term and nothing to buy back. A studio that has to trap you isn't confident about the work.
